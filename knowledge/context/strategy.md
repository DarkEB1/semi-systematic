## Thesis

Markets are an aggregate picture of the mapping of price to value. We use quantitative signals,
models and constructs to read what expectations are encoded in prices, compare them with our own
value judgements, and bet on the discrepancies in a rigorously defined manner. The systematic side
anchors how we look at and across markets; the discretionary side supplies the judgements. We want
to build a view, not learn a view.

## Scope

Preliminarily EM and FX, restricted by the data available for us to form opinions. See
[universe](universe.md) for the instruments and [conventions](conventions.md) for how returns,
costs, calendars and currencies are defined.

## What a good trade looks like

A clear statement of the market's implied expectation, a defensible value judgement that differs
from it, a model that links the two, and an asymmetric return distribution once costs and risk are
counted. Everything else is research.
